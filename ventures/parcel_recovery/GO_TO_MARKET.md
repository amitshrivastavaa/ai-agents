# Parcel Recovery — 90-day plan to first revenue

The goal of the first 90 days is **one thing: get the first contingency check.**
Not a platform, not scale — one client whose money you recovered and who paid you a
cut. Everything below serves that.

## The offer (memorize this)
> "Send me 12 months of your FedEx/UPS invoices. I'll show you exactly what's
> recoverable — **free**. If you like the number, I file the claims and take **20%
> of what actually lands back in your account.** No recovery, no fee."

Why it works: zero risk to the buyer, the money is real, and you only need their
data — not their trust — to produce the wow moment (the audit report).

## Who to sell to (ICP)
**Mid-market shippers spending $250k–$5M/year on parcel** — big enough to leak real
money ($35k+/yr recoverable per $500k of spend), too small for white-glove auditors
(PRGX/Deloitte) to chase.

Concretely, target:
- E-commerce brands & DTC (Shopify/Amazon sellers past ~$3M GMV)
- 3PLs and fulfillment houses (they ship for many clients → leverage)
- Distributors / wholesalers with daily outbound freight
- Furniture, parts, and other heavy/dimensional shippers (dim-weight errors compound)

Disqualify: shippers under ~$150k/yr spend (recovery too small to be worth your time)
and anyone already on a parcel-audit contract (re-approach at renewal).

## Channels (no sales army required)
1. **Free-audit outbound.** Your own engine is the lead magnet. Identify likely-leaky
   shippers (high volume, heavy/dimensional goods), and lead the cold email/DM with a
   number: *"I estimate ~$X,000/yr recoverable from your FedEx account — want the free
   audit to confirm?"* The audit report **is** the pitch.
2. **Channel partners who already hold the relationship** — bookkeepers, fractional
   CFOs, 3PLs, ERP/AP-software resellers, e-commerce agencies. Give them 10–20% rev-share
   per closed client. One good partner = a pipeline.
3. **Vertical niche-down.** Pick ONE vertical first ("parcel recovery for furniture
   e-commerce"). Referenceable wins → word of mouth → tailored playbook → you become
   "the parcel-recovery person" for that niche.
4. **Communities & content.** Logistics/e-comm subreddits, Slacks, LinkedIn. Post real
   teardowns ("here's $4,200 a shipper left on the table last quarter"). Specific
   numbers travel.

## Pricing
- **20–25% contingency** on recovered funds (undercut the 50%-of-savings incumbents).
- No setup fee, no minimum, no lock-in for the first 5 clients — remove every reason to
  say no while you build proof.
- Later: tiered contingency by volume, or a low monthly base + lower % for big accounts.

## The 90 days

### Days 1–21 — Sharpen the wedge & line up data
- Pick the beachhead vertical and the recovery categories you'll lead with (late-delivery
  + dim-weight are the highest-$, clearest-cut — start there).
- Build the carrier-invoice ingest for **one** carrier first (FedEx **or** UPS billing
  export → the CSV this engine reads). Manual is fine.
- Write the free-audit report template (this repo's output is the v0).
- Line up **10 target shippers** + **3 potential channel partners** by name.

### Days 22–45 — Run free audits
- Get invoice data from **5 prospects** (the free audit is the ask). Run the engine, hand
  each a personalized recoverable number + filing pack.
- **Benchmark to proceed:** at least 3 of 5 audits surface a recoverable figure big enough
  to act on (target ≥ $5k/yr). If they don't, your ICP is too small — move upmarket.

### Days 46–75 — File and recover
- Convert ≥2 audited prospects to signed contingency clients.
- **File the claims** (this is the unglamorous core — carrier dispute portals, GSR claims,
  duplicate/dim disputes). Track every claim's status and outcome.
- Instrument the **outcome data from day one**: which claim types win, per carrier, at what
  rate. That dataset is the start of the moat.

### Days 76–90 — First check + the flywheel
- **Land the first recovered funds → invoice your contingency %.** That's the milestone.
- Ask each happy client for (a) a referral and (b) permission to auto-run monthly (recovery
  is recurring — carriers keep making the same errors).
- Sign your first channel partner on rev-share.

## Success = one paid recovery
By day 90: **1–3 contingency clients, the first recovered check in hand, and an outcome
dataset growing.** That proves the whole thesis on near-zero cash. From there the moat is
account expansion (add AP, telecom, freight audit to the same clients — "recovery-as-a-
service") and the compounding per-carrier outcome data.

## What kills it (watch these)
- **ICP too small** → audits surface trivial amounts. Fix: move upmarket in spend.
- **Filing is the real work** → detection is easy; the grind is carrier portals, deadlines,
  and follow-up. Budget time for it; it's also the barrier that protects you.
- **Crowded lanes** → don't lead with chargebacks (AI-native incumbents, $100M-funded).
  Parcel/AP/expense recovery, where incumbents are human-labor shops, is the opening.
